Vitamin C vs Niacinamide: which serum fits your brand?

Vitamin C is the brightening story; Niacinamide is the calm, barrier-friendly story. Most brands start with Niacinamide because it suits more skin types.

What MOQ 500–1,000 really means

MOQ 500–1,000 means 500–1,000 units of one SKU — one formula, in one pack, for one market. It is the smallest bulk order; samples are separate.
