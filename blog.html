Each article starts with a direct answer to a question buyers actually ask, then explains the detail. No fixed prices, no invented factory numbers — only what is confirmed, with what is still to be confirmed marked as such.

How to choose a private label serum formula

Start with your market and your hero ingredient, not with a long ingredient list. A ready formula is fastest; a custom formula fits a brand that needs its own story.

Formula

Vitamin C vs Niacinamide: which serum fits your brand?

Vitamin C is the brightening story; Niacinamide is the calm, barrier-friendly story. Most brands start with Niacinamide because it suits more skin types.

6 Nov 2026

MOQ

What MOQ 1,000 really means

MOQ 1,000 means 1,000 units of one SKU — one formula, in one pack, for one market. It is the smallest bulk order; samples are separate.

6 Nov 2026

Sampling

How sampling works before bulk production

You approve a sample before bulk production starts. Send feedback, the formula is adjusted if needed, and only the confirmed sample goes to bulk.

Packaging

Private label skincare packaging options, explained

Serums take dropper or pump bottles, creams take jars or airless pumps, cleansers take tubes or pump bottles. Confirm format, size and artwork before production.

ISO 22716 certification: what it means for your market

ISO 22716 is the international cosmetics GMP standard. Amy Skin's verified claim is SGS ISO 22716 (Sanmusheng) — it supports registration, but does not replace it.
