S4 · MOQ

MOQ 1,000 units per SKU

One SKU is one formula, in one pack, for one market. MOQ 1,000 is the smallest bulk order for that one product.

  • Per SKU, not per order: two products need 1,000 units each.
  • Samples are separate: you approve a sample before the 1,000 units are made.
  • Price per order: the price depends on formula, pack and quantity — quoted in private, never fixed here.
