Frequently asked questions

Questions buyers ask first

What is the MOQ?

1,000 units per SKU — one formula, in one pack, for one market. Samples are separate from the MOQ. Sample fee and lead time are confirmed per order.

Can I get a sample before bulk production?

Yes. You approve a sample before bulk production starts. Sample fee and lead time are confirmed per order.

Which certification do you hold?

The verified claim is SGS ISO 22716 (Sanmusheng). Other factory certifications are to be verified.

How long does an order take?

Lead time is confirmed per order, because it depends on the formula, the packaging and the quantity. No fixed lead time is published on this website.
