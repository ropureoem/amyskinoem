FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the MOQ?

500–1,000 units per SKU — one formula, in one pack, for one market. Prices are quoted per order.

Can I start from a ready formula?

Yes. Start from our 10,000+ formula library — or one of 34 featured formulas — customise it with the factory team, then approve a sample before bulk production. There are no standard off-the-shelf products.

Can I get an exclusive formula?

Yes — at our factory, exclusive formulas are a standard service, and most larger brands ask for one. There are three kinds: an exclusive formula (the same formula is never made for another brand), an exclusive variant (your version of a formula is yours alone), and market or channel exclusivity (the formula is only supplied to you in your market or channel). Differentiation is straightforward: adjusting equivalent ingredients creates a different formula with the same performance — that is how we deliver exclusivity in practice. That is the charm of skincare. Terms, including any link to MOQ or a development fee, are confirmed per order.

Why do you only show 34 formulas?

Our library of 10,000+ formulas is proprietary — formulas are trade secrets. Share your brief and we will recommend the right starting formulas for your brand; an NDA can be signed where needed.

How does sampling work?

You approve a sample before bulk production starts. Send your brief, receive a sample of the formula, test it and give feedback — only a sample you confirm goes into bulk production.

What is the lead time?

Lead time is confirmed per order — it depends on the formula, packaging and quantity you choose.
