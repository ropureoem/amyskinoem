Who is Amy Skin OEM?

Amy Skin is a private label skincare OEM / ODM brand made in our factory in Guangzhou, China. We help new and growing brands launch skincare with ready formulas or custom development, starting at MOQ 500–1,000 units per SKU.

Real factory and laboratory photos are added when available — no stock or third-party factory photos are used on this site.

Our promise: 3A

Arrives Reliable. Great skin, honest prices — and production you can track step by step.

Always Relaxed. Low MOQ. Skincare made simple. OEM / ODM services that are actually accessible to small brands.

Actually Affordable. Competitive and honest pricing — quoted per order, not fixed on this website, because the right price depends on formula, pack and quantity.

Where we come from

Our factory partners go back further than the Amy Skin name. Wangsha Group was founded in 2006 in Huadu, Guangzhou, and built a 60,000 m² production base in Qingyuan; Sanmusheng was founded in 2011 in Guangzhou. Amy Skin brings their production together under one private-label brand.
