What is a private label centella amino acid skin care set?

A complete six-step routine in one retail box: an amino acid cleanser, a centella toner, an ampoule serum, an eye cream, a face cream and a wash-off mask, covering day and night. Centella plus panthenol calms redness and rebuilds a stressed barrier, while niacinamide and collagen even dull tone and improve bounce.
