Which certifications does your market need?

Our one verified claim is SGS ISO 22716 (Sanmusheng). ISO 22716 is the international Good Manufacturing Practices standard for cosmetics: it means production, hygiene, quality control and documentation follow that standard. It supports your market registration, but it does not by itself put your product on the market — registration or notification, and a safety assessment where required, are separate steps.
