How to use the kit

Use the Ink logo on light backgrounds and the White logo on Ink. Keep one cap height of “A” as clear space on all sides. Do not recolour, re-space, stretch or compress the wordmark, and do not place it over busy photography. Print minimum 20 mm wide; screen minimum 100 px wide, never below 60 px. For the full rules, see the brand guide and the one-page VI standard sheet above.
