How to use the kit

Use the primary lockup on light backgrounds and the reversed lockup on #0A0908. Reversed is for black only — header, footer, black hero / CTA areas, black packs. Primary is for light ground only. Keep one cap height of “A” as clear space on all sides. The wordmark is a custom-drawn logotype — do not re-typeset, recolour, re-space, stretch or compress it, and do not place it over busy photography. Print minimum 20 mm wide; screen minimum 100 px wide, never below 60 px — use the droplet-A mark alone at small sizes. For the full rules, see the brand guide and the one-page VI standard sheet above.
