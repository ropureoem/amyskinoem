How to choose a private label serum formula?

Start with your market and your hero ingredient, not with a long ingredient list. Pick one clear story — brightening, hydration or anti-aging — then choose a ready formula or a custom formula that tells that story, and approve a sample before bulk production.

What should you decide first?

Three things. First, your market: EU, USA, Middle East or elsewhere, because the certification and claim rules differ. Second, the one skin concern your serum is for — brightness, dryness or firmness. Third, your hero ingredient: Vitamin C for brightening, Niacinamide for gentle brightening, Hyaluronic Acid for hydration, or Peptide for anti-aging.

Ready formula or custom formula?

OptionWhen it fitsWhat to expect
Ready formulaYou want the fastest launchAlready developed; go straight to sampling and packaging
Custom formulaYou need your own texture, scent or ingredient storyDeveloped with the factory team; takes more time

Most new brands start with a ready formula. Our 12 ready formulas include four serums: Vitamin C, Hyaluronic Acid, Niacinamide and Peptide.

What does a good serum brief include?

  • Your target market and the claims it allows
  • Your hero ingredient and the story you want to tell
  • Texture you prefer — light, rich, or in between
  • Pack format: dropper or pump bottle
  • Quantity per SKU (MOQ 1,000)

What happens after you choose?

You follow the 5-step OEM / ODM process: brief, formula and sampling, packaging confirmation, production, delivery. You approve the sample before bulk production — see how sampling works.
