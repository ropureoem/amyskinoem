6 Nov 2026 · Amy Skin OEM Insights

What packaging can your skincare brand choose?

Dropper bottles and pump bottles for serums, jars and airless pumps for creams, tubes and pump bottles for cleansers, and sealed pouches for sheet masks. Outer boxes and printed labels carry your brand. Confirm the format, size and artwork before production starts.

Which pack fits which formula?

ProductUsual packWhy
SerumDropper or pump bottlePrecise dosing; protects the formula
CreamJar or airless pumpEasy to use; airless protects from air
CleanserTube or pump bottleLight, easy to dispense
Sheet maskSealed pouchSingle-use, hygienic
Body lotion / shampooPump bottle or tubeEasy daily use
SunscreenTube or pump bottleEasy to carry and apply

What about the box and the label?

The outer box and the printed label carry your brand name, product name, directions and the claims your market allows. Your artwork is approved before printing. See Brand & visual identity for our logo and colour rules, and the packaging page for the full list of formats.

What is not fixed yet?

Specific supplier pack ranges, exact sizes and pack minimums are to be confirmed — the formats above are the families to discuss, and exact availability is confirmed per order with the factory team. Send your pack idea with your quote brief, and it will be confirmed before you approve production.
