Vitamin C vs Niacinamide: which serum fits your brand?

Vitamin C is the brightening story; Niacinamide is the calm, barrier-friendly story. Most new brands start with Niacinamide because it suits more skin types — then add Vitamin C as a second serum when the brand is ready.

How do they compare?

Vitamin C SerumNiacinamide Serum
StoryBrightening, glowGentle brightening, pore care
Strength10% / 15% Vitamin CBarrier-friendly, suits most skin types
Best first serum?Yes, if your brand is about glowYes, for most new brands
MOQ500–1,000 units per SKU500–1,000 units per SKU

Which one should you launch first?

Ask one question: what is the one sentence your brand says about this serum? If the sentence is about glow and brightness, choose Vitamin C Serum. If it is about gentle, everyday care that most customers can use, choose Niacinamide Serum. You can launch both — each is one SKU at MOQ 500–1,000 — but launch the clearer story first.

Can you combine them in one formula?

That is a custom-formula question. Tell us the story you want and the claims you sell in, and the factory team will confirm whether a combined direction fits your market's rules. Either way, you approve a sample before bulk production — see how sampling works.
